Privacy Policy for Chess Timer

Effective Date: November 2026

Welcome to Royal Chess Clock / Chess Timer ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the Chess Timer mobile application on Android.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (Offline-First)

We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, or GPS location) to any external servers.

All application settings, sound preferences, player profile aliases, and game history records are stored strictly locally on your device using local device storage (AsyncStorage) and never leave your phone.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the application free for everyone, we use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain technical identifiers and analytics:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) used for frequency capping, reporting, and ad attribution.
  • Device & Network Data: Device model, OS version, screen size, IP address, and network connection status.
  • Ad Telemetry: Ad impressions, banner clicks, and diagnostic performance data.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

3. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to fetch and render banner advertisements from Google AdMob.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when tapping the clock buttons during play.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used solely to prevent your screen from going to sleep while a chess match is actively running.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Used to play tick sounds and low-time warning alerts through your device speaker.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The application is a utility tool designed for chess players of all ages.

5. Data Deletion Instructions

Since all match history and profiles are stored purely on your physical device, you can completely and permanently delete all data at any moment by:

  • Using the "Reset / Clear History" option in the app's settings, OR
  • Clearing App Data / Storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Chess Timer → Storage → Clear Data), OR
  • Uninstalling the app from your device.
